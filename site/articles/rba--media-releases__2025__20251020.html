2025

The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) has maintained a consistent flow of policy communications and operational updates throughout 2025, underscoring its ongoing efforts to support financial stability and efficient market functioning. These actions are critical as they shape Australia’s economic environment and financial infrastructure amid evolving market conditions.

Throughout 2025, the RBA issued numerous media releases covering a range of monetary policy decisions, regulatory actions, and collaborative initiatives. These include periodic statements on monetary policy decisions by the Monetary Policy Board, reflecting responses to economic indicators and inflation targets. For instance, official releases dated 18 February, 1 April, 12 August, and 30 September indicate the Bank’s active use of monetary policy tools to guide economic outcomes.

The RBA also engaged in forward-looking consultations regarding payment systems and clearing facilities, signalling an emphasis on enhancing the resilience and efficiency of Australia’s financial infrastructure. Releases in mid-2025 highlight consultations on the future of the account-to-account payments system and guidance for clearing and settlement facility resolution regimes. These measures aim to future-proof the payments ecosystem against operational risks and technological shifts.

Regulatory oversight has remained a priority, as noted in media releases addressing the assessment and licensing of clearing and settlement facilities. The granting of an overseas clearing and settlement facility licence to Clearstream Banking S.A. on 18 July 2025 exemplifies the RBA’s role in facilitating cross-border financial market integration while maintaining robust risk controls.

The Bank’s collaboration with other regulatory bodies is evident in joint efforts to strengthen financial system stability. On 29 July 2025, the RBA and the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) updated their memorandum of understanding to enhance cooperation. This coordinated approach supports early identification and mitigation of systemic risks, reinforcing public confidence in the financial system.

Additionally, the RBA advanced research initiatives exploring innovative financial technologies. A notable example is “Project Acacia,” announced on 10 July 2025, involving industry participants and regulatory relief from the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) to test tokenised asset settlement solutions. This project reflects the Bank’s commitment to fostering innovation while ensuring regulatory compliance and investor protection.

The Institution also maintained bilateral currency swap agreements to support financial market liquidity and currency stability. Renewals with the Bank of Japan and Bank Indonesia underscore the RBA's role in preserving smooth international financial relations and mitigating potential shocks in times of volatility.

Symbolic initiatives were not neglected. The release of a $5 banknote theme celebrating the First Nations connection to country, announced on 17 March 2025, highlights the Bank’s recognition of cultural heritage and social inclusion alongside its economic mandates.

Apart from these concrete steps, the RBA continued to provide transparency and accessibility through public appointments and stakeholder engagement activities. Staff announcements scattered across the year reflect ongoing organisational adjustments to support the Bank’s strategic objectives.

The RBA’s prolific communications in 2025 collectively demonstrate its multifaceted approach to monetary policy, financial regulation, innovation, and cultural recognition. By balancing these roles, the Bank aims to foster a stable and adaptable financial environment, crucial for Australia’s economic health in an increasingly complex global landscape.
