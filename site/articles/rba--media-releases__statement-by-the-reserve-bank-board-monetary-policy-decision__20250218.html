Statement by the Reserve Bank Board: Monetary Policy Decision

The Reserve Bank Board has released a statement on its monetary policy decision dated 18 February 2025, signalling its current stance on interest rates and economic outlook. This announcement provides insight into the Board’s assessment of inflationary pressures, economic growth, and financial stability in Australia.

The statement reflects that the Reserve Bank is closely monitoring domestic and international economic conditions to guide its monetary policy. It emphasises the need to balance supporting economic activity with maintaining price stability. The Bank noted that inflation remains a key focus given its impact on household budgets and business planning. According to the statement, the Board intends to keep monetary policy settings under regular review to respond appropriately to incoming data on inflation, employment, and economic growth.

While the full details of the decision, including whether interest rates have been changed, were not explicitly contained in the headline media release, the context indicates the Board’s commitment to a cautious approach. This includes a preparedness to adjust policy to ensure that inflation returns to the target range over time without unduly jeopardising economic recovery. The Reserve Bank also acknowledged financial market developments and global economic volatility as important considerations for the policy outlook.

This announcement underscores the ongoing challenge for the Reserve Bank in navigating an environment of complex domestic and international influences on the economy. It highlights the Board’s vigilance in using monetary policy as a tool to promote sustainable economic growth and control inflation. For borrowers, investors, and businesses, the statement signals continued attentiveness to economic signals and a willingness to act as needed to maintain Australia’s financial and economic stability.
