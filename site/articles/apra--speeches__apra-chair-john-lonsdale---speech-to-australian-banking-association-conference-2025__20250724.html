APRA Chair John Lonsdale - Speech to Australian Banking Association Conference 2025

APRA Chair John Lonsdale outlined the scope and responsibility of the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) in his 24 July 2025 address to the Australian Banking Association Conference. His remarks highlighted APRA’s critical role in ensuring the safety and soundness of the financial services sector, which directly affects millions of Australians.

Lonsdale emphasised that APRA acts as the prudential regulator for a broad range of financial institutions. This includes banks, mutuals, general insurance and reinsurance companies, life insurance providers, private health insurers, friendly societies, and much of the superannuation industry. By supervising these entities, APRA safeguards the stability of financial services that underpin depositors, policyholders, and superannuation fund members.

According to Lonsdale, APRA currently regulates institutions holding approximately $9.8 trillion in assets. These assets represent the collective wealth and financial security of Australian consumers. The sheer scale of these holdings underlines APRA’s vital responsibility to ensure institutions remain resilient and capable of meeting their financial obligations.

Lonsdale’s speech reaffirmed APRA’s commitment to prudential oversight aimed at protecting the interests of the Australian public. Through rigorous supervision, APRA seeks to reduce risks that could threaten financial system stability or undermine consumer confidence. The authority’s broad mandate reflects the complexity and interconnectedness of the financial sector, requiring comprehensive and vigilant regulation.

In conclusion, APRA’s role as described by Chair Lonsdale underscores its essential function in maintaining trust and soundness within Australia’s financial system. By overseeing a wide array of financial institutions managing trillions in assets, APRA plays a central role in fostering resilience and safeguarding the financial well-being of Australians now and into the future.
