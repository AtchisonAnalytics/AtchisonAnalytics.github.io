Joint APRA-RBA Statement on Use of the RBA’s Overnight Standing Facility

The Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) and the Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) issued a joint statement on 2 April 2025, addressing the use of the RBA’s Overnight Standing Facility. This communication clarifies the roles and guidance related to the facility, signalling coordinated oversight intended to support financial stability and efficient market functioning.

The RBA’s Overnight Standing Facility allows eligible financial institutions to obtain overnight liquidity directly from the central bank, typically used to smooth short-term funding pressures and ensure market confidence. APRA and the RBA emphasised that this facility is not designed for routine funding needs but acts as a backstop during periods of market stress or unexpected liquidity shortfalls. The statement highlights that prudent liquidity management remains the responsibility of individual institutions, under APRA’s supervisory framework.

The joint statement outlined that the RBA maintains operational control of the Overnight Standing Facility, setting the interest rate and eligibility criteria, while APRA focuses on prudential supervision to ensure institutions maintain adequate liquidity buffers. APRA’s role includes monitoring usage patterns to detect early signs of stress or broader financial system risks. Both authorities stressed the importance of transparent communication with market participants to reinforce confidence in the facility’s role as a stabilising tool rather than a primary funding source.

APRA and the RBA also reiterated their commitment to ongoing collaboration and information sharing to enhance the resilience of the Australian financial system. The statement underlined that the coexistence of supervisory oversight and central bank operations allows for timely interventions, should systemic liquidity issues arise. Such cooperation seeks to prevent contagion effects and support orderly market functioning during periods of heightened volatility.

Although the source material lacks specific operational or technical details, the joint communiqué reinforces the authorities’ unified approach to managing liquidity risks and safeguarding the integrity of Australia’s financial markets. By clarifying responsibilities and expectations around the Overnight Standing Facility, APRA and the RBA aim to strengthen institutional discipline and public confidence.

This statement signals a clear message to financial institutions that liquidity management is a critical prudential obligation, with the RBA’s Overnight Standing Facility available as a carefully regulated fallback mechanism. The ongoing partnership between APRA and the RBA ensures that liquidity support is timely, targeted, and consistent with sound monetary and regulatory policy objectives, thereby enhancing the stability of the Australian financial system.
