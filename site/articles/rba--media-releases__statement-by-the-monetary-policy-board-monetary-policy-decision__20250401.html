Statement by the Monetary Policy Board: Monetary Policy Decision

On 1 April 2025, the Reserve Bank of Australia’s Monetary Policy Board announced its latest monetary policy decision, reflecting its ongoing assessment of economic conditions and inflation risks. This decision is critical as it guides financial markets, influences borrowing costs, and helps shape Australia’s economic outlook.

The Board reaffirmed its commitment to achieving the inflation target, balancing the need to support economic growth while managing inflationary pressures. The statement indicated that the global economic environment remains uncertain, with ongoing supply chain disruptions and varying international monetary policy responses contributing to this volatility. Domestically, the economy is gradually returning to trend growth, supported by positive employment data and steady consumer spending.

The Board acknowledged that inflation remains elevated above the target band. It noted that while headline inflation has peaked, underlying inflationary pressures require vigilance. Household spending and business investment have shown resilience, yet wage growth remains moderate, tempering inflation expectations. The Bank emphasised that inflation forecasts hinge on the trajectory of wages, global commodity prices, and supply bottlenecks.

Further, the Board highlighted the importance of cautious monetary settings to ensure inflation returns sustainably to the 2 to 3 per cent target over time. It confirmed that the official cash rate remains a primary tool to influence economic activity and inflation. The decision on the cash rate was informed by comprehensive analysis of domestic and international economic indicators, credit conditions, and labour market trends.

The Reserve Bank’s statement also referenced financial market developments, noting stable exchange rates and long-term interest rates. The Board observed that market pricing of future monetary policy adjustments appears broadly consistent with a gradual approach. This measured stance aims to support continued economic recovery without triggering undue volatility or financial instability.

In conclusion, the Monetary Policy Board reiterated its readiness to adjust monetary policy settings as required to deliver on the inflation target while fostering sustainable economic growth and employment. The decision signals a cautious but steady approach amid a complex global and domestic environment. For businesses, investors, and households, this statement underscores the importance of monitoring evolving conditions and the Reserve Bank’s vigilant response to achieve economic stability.
