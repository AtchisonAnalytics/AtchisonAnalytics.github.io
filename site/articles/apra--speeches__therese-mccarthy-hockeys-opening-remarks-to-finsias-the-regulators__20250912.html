Therese McCarthy Hockey’s opening remarks to FINSIA’s The Regulators

Therese McCarthy Hockey addressed FINSIA’s The Regulators event on 12 September 2025, highlighting the critical role of the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA) in maintaining financial system stability. Her remarks underscored APRA’s broad supervisory responsibilities and the scale of assets it oversees, emphasising its importance to Australia’s economic wellbeing.

APRA acts as the prudential regulator for Australia’s financial services industry, with a mandate to ensure the safety and soundness of regulated institutions. According to McCarthy Hockey, APRA’s remit includes banks, mutuals, general insurance and reinsurance companies, life insurers, private health insurers, friendly societies, and most members of the superannuation industry. These sectors collectively manage substantial portions of Australian household wealth and savings.

At present, APRA supervises institutions that hold approximately $9.8 trillion in assets. This figure represents the financial interests of Australian depositors, policyholders, and superannuation fund members. McCarthy Hockey’s remarks highlighted that this vast stewardship imposes a direct responsibility on APRA to safeguard the integrity and resilience of these institutions against potential risks.

The regulator operates within a framework designed to mitigate threats to the financial system while fostering confidence among consumers and investors. McCarthy Hockey’s opening comments suggested that APRA’s role extends beyond routine supervision to active engagement in industry developments and emerging challenges. She implied that continual adaptation and rigorous oversight are necessary to keep pace with changes in financial markets and economic conditions.

References during her speech to collaborative reviews by the Council of Financial Regulators (CFR) and the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) indicate APRA’s interconnected role in the broader regulatory landscape. This cooperation highlights a collective effort to maintain competitive, fair, and stable financial systems in Australia.

For stakeholders and members of the Australian financial system, McCarthy Hockey’s address reaffirmed the centrality of APRA’s prudential supervision. The scale and scope of the institution’s oversight functions demonstrate its pivotal position in protecting consumer funds and sustaining trust in the financial sector. As regulatory challenges evolve, APRA’s stewardship will remain essential to Australia’s economic security and the resilience of its financial institutions.
