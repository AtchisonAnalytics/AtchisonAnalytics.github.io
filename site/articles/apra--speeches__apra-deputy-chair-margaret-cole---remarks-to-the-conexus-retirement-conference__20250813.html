APRA Deputy Chair Margaret Cole - Remarks to the Conexus Retirement Conference

APRA Deputy Chair Margaret Cole addressed the Conexus Retirement Conference on 13 August 2025, outlining the scope and regulatory role of the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority. Her remarks highlighted APRA’s significant oversight of the financial sector and the importance of prudential regulation in protecting Australia’s financial system.

Cole emphasised that APRA is the prudential regulator responsible for a broad range of financial services institutions, including banks, mutuals, general insurers and reinsurers, life insurers, private health insurers, and friendly societies. Importantly, APRA also supervises most members of the superannuation industry, which is critical for Australians’ retirement savings and financial security. According to Cole, APRA oversees institutions holding assets worth approximately $9.8 trillion, safeguarding the interests of depositors, policyholders, and superannuation fund members alike.

Her remarks underscored APRA’s comprehensive regulatory framework designed to promote the safety and soundness of institutions managing billions of dollars on behalf of Australians. By maintaining stringent prudential standards, APRA aims to ensure these entities remain resilient to financial shocks and can meet their obligations to consumers over the long term. This oversight helps foster confidence in the financial system and supports the stability of Australia’s economy.

Cole’s speech also implicitly highlighted the interconnected nature of the financial services industry under APRA’s remit, where the prudential health of one sector can affect others. With nearly $10 trillion in assets under supervision, APRA’s role is pivotal in managing systemic risks and guiding institutions through evolving economic conditions and regulatory expectations.

In summary, Margaret Cole’s remarks to the conference reaffirm APRA’s critical position in overseeing a vast segment of Australia’s financial services industry. Her statements reflect the authority’s commitment to protecting stakeholders and maintaining a stable, trustworthy financial environment as Australia’s economy and retirement landscape continue to evolve.
