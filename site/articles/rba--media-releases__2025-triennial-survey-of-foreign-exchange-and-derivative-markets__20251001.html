2025 Triennial Survey of Foreign Exchange and Derivative Markets

The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) is set to publish the results of the 2025 Triennial Survey of Foreign Exchange and Derivative Markets, a key measure of Australia's financial market activity. This survey captures transaction volumes and liquidity trends, providing critical insights into the size and structure of the domestic and global foreign exchange and derivatives markets.

The triennial survey assesses turnover in foreign exchange (FX) and over-the-counter (OTC) derivative markets, helping policymakers and market participants understand shifts in demand and behaviour. Historically, this survey offers a comprehensive snapshot of trading volumes, counterparties involved, and currency pairs most actively traded. These metrics enable the RBA and market analysts to monitor evolving market dynamics, assess systemic risks, and calibrate regulatory responses.

According to past RBA surveys, FX trading is dominated by major currencies such as the US dollar, euro, and Japanese yen. The Australian dollar, given its status as a commodity-linked currency, also features prominently. Trading volumes in the derivatives market often reflect firms’ hedging strategies against interest rate, credit, and currency risk exposures. Changes in these patterns across survey periods indicate shifts in macroeconomic conditions and policy settings, such as monetary tightening or easing by major central banks.

While the detailed 2025 results have yet to be released, the RBA’s continued commitment to the triennial survey signifies its importance as a barometer of market liquidity and risk distribution. The findings will be crucial for understanding the implications of persistent global economic uncertainties, shifting monetary policies, and technological advances in market infrastructure.

The survey’s data also support financial stability assessments and ensure Australia’s alignment with international standards, as similar surveys are conducted by central banks worldwide under the auspices of the Bank for International Settlements. Greater transparency and data accuracy from the survey inform effective supervision of foreign exchange and derivative markets, which are foundational to Australia's financial system resilience.

For market participants, policymakers, and researchers, the 2025 Triennial Survey will offer a timely update on trading behaviours and emerging trends within global and domestic FX and derivative markets. Its release will augment understanding of how global events impact Australian markets and guide prudent risk management. The ongoing collection and analysis of such comprehensive data underscore the RBA’s role in promoting a stable and efficient financial marketplace.
