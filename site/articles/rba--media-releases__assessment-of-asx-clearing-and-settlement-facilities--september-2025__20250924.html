Assessment of ASX Clearing and Settlement Facilities – September 2025

The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) has published its assessment of the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) clearing and settlement facilities as of September 2025. This review is crucial for ensuring the stability and efficiency of systems that underpin Australia’s financial markets.

The RBA oversees the ASX clearing and settlement facilities to confirm they achieve appropriate standards of risk management, operational efficiency, and resilience. While the specific findings of the September 2025 review have not been detailed in the publicly available media release, the Bank’s ongoing scrutiny reflects its mandate to promote the robustness of Australia's payment and settlement infrastructure. These systems support the exchange of securities and cash, which is central to market confidence and the smooth functioning of the broader financial system.

The ASX facilities form critical market infrastructure tasked with managing counterparty risk and ensuring timely finality in transactions. The RBA’s assessments typically analyse compliance with applicable regulations, operational risk controls, and the sufficiency of safeguards against financial shocks. The RBA also examines the technological resilience of the systems, such as their ability to operate amid cyber threats or extreme market stress, which reflects the Bank’s broader commitment to financial stability.

Although the 2025 assessment document itself is not available in detail, RBA releases historically emphasise the importance of continuous enhancement of these facilities. The Bank often recommends that ASX maintain rigorous stress testing and contingency planning, to mitigate potential disruptions. The ASX clearing and settlement facilities, in this context, are expected to evolve in response to technological advances, regulatory changes, and emerging risks.

Financial market participants, regulators, and policymakers closely monitor such assessments as they provide a benchmark for operational soundness and offer transparency on how systemic risks are managed. The resilience of these core infrastructures directly impacts market participants’ confidence and the integrity of capital markets in Australia.

The Reserve Bank’s commitment to robust assessments underscores the central role of clearing and settlement systems in safeguarding the Australian economy from financial shocks. As these facilities adapt to an evolving market and regulatory landscape, ongoing oversight will remain vital to ensure they sustain their fundamental role in Australia’s financial stability.
